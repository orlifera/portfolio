# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two readers, served equally:

- **Recruiters and hiring teams** sizing up a fullstack developer. They skim on a laptop between other tabs and want projects, experience and a CV within a minute.
- **Freelance clients**: businesses and professionals, mostly around Veneto, who need a website or mobile app. Often on a phone, they want to know what is offered, roughly what it costs, and how to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Orlando V. M. Ferazzani, fullstack developer (React, Next.js, TypeScript, React Native) based in Padua. It shows his projects and experience, explains his freelance services and pricing, and makes contact easy (form, email, phone, WhatsApp, CV download). Success is a message sent or a CV downloaded.

## Brand Personality

Playful, direct, hands-on. First-person and informal ("Hello 🤙🏽 I'm", "I promise I'll be quick", "It's not much, but it's honest work"), never corporate.

The differentiator is the diving side: Orlando has been a Divemaster and deep scuba guide since 2019. That brings calm competence under pressure, and a thrill-seeker who does not compromise on safety. The site should leave a visitor curious, trusting, and smiling.

Professional before playful in how it looks. The scuba side comes through the photography, the light and the instrument detail, never through cartoon elements such as drawn waves or bubbles.

## Anti-references

- The template developer portfolio: dark mode with neon purple/pink gradient glows and stock component-library effects left at their defaults.
- SaaS landing clichés: hero metrics, identical icon-card grids, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows over every section.
- A cartoon ocean theme: clip-art fish, anchors, wave emoji. Diving is carried by light, colour, depth and instruments, not illustration.

## Design Principles

1. **One dive, top to bottom.** The page is a descent: scroll position is depth, and colour, light and type width all follow from it.
2. **Practice what you sell.** The site sells fast, accessible websites, so it has to be one: contrast, keyboard access, reduced motion and performance are part of the pitch.
3. **The person before the stack.** Real photos and a first-person voice lead; tech logos and tags support.
4. **Instruments, not ornaments.** Motion and chrome report state (depth, progress, focus) the way a dive computer does. Nothing moves only to move.
5. **Serve both readers.** Recruiters reach projects, experience and the CV fast; clients reach services, prices and contact fast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA as the floor, with AAA contrast for body text where feasible (the services promise AAA to clients). Everything is keyboard operable with a visible focus state, every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative, and no information is available on hover alone.
